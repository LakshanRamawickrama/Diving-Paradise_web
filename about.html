About Diving Paradise Mirissa

Passionate about the ocean, dedicated to safety, and committed to creating memories that last a lifetime — discover the story and the team behind Mirissa's #1 rated marine adventure center.

Our Story

Discover the Beauty
Beneath the Waves

Welcome to Diving Paradise Mirissa — where adventure meets the breathtaking beauty of Sri Lanka's spectacular southern coastline. Nestled in the iconic coastal town of Mirissa, we are a passionate team of ocean lovers dedicated to helping every visitor discover the incredible underwater world of the Indian Ocean.

Whether you are a first-time snorkeler, an experienced scuba diver, a family on holiday, or simply someone searching for an unforgettable ocean adventure — our professional, PADI-certified guides are here to ensure your experience is safe, exhilarating, and deeply memorable.

From the very first moment you step into our dive center at No.21 Athkam Niwasa, Mirissa Beach, you become part of a family that shares one simple mission: to connect people with the awe-inspiring beauty of the sea.

Who We Are

A PADI-Certified Team
Passionate About the Ocean

Diving Paradise Mirissa is dedicated to providing high-quality diving, snorkeling, and ocean experiences for travelers from all corners of the world. We are proud to be one of the most trusted and highly rated ocean adventure operators on Sri Lanka's southern coast.

Our experienced team combines deep professional knowledge with genuine warmth and friendly service, ensuring every guest — regardless of experience level — feels comfortable and confident throughout their time with us. We hold the highest standards of safety and maintain our equipment to professional dive industry specifications.

We believe that diving is not just about exploring underwater worlds — it is about forging unforgettable memories, building a connection with the marine environment, and returning home with stories that inspire others. From colorful tropical fish and vibrant coral reefs to majestic sea turtles and playful dolphins, the waters around Mirissa are filled with magic — and we are here to share it with you.

Who Can Join Us

Our Adventures Are For Everyone

Whether you have never seen the ocean or are a seasoned dive professional — there is a perfect experience waiting for you at Diving Paradise Mirissa.

First-Time Visitors & Non-Swimmers
Never snorkeled or swum in the ocean before? Our guides provide dedicated 1-on-1 support, floating vests, and constant supervision so you can safely enjoy the sea — no prior experience needed.
Families & Children
We warmly welcome families with children. Our snorkeling and boat tours are gentle, fun, and perfectly suited for kids who want to discover ocean life in a safe, guided environment.
Solo Travelers & Backpackers
Traveling alone and looking for an adventure? Join one of our group departures to meet fellow travelers while experiencing the most spectacular marine life in Sri Lanka's southern waters.
Certified Scuba Divers
Already certified? Join our Fun Dives, Wreck Diving Adventures, or explore Mirissa's diverse reef systems with our PADI-certified divemasters who know every inch of the local dive sites.
Aspiring Divers & Students
Want to learn to dive properly? From Discover Scuba to the full PADI Open Water, Advanced, Rescue, and Divemaster courses — we offer a complete training pathway in a stunning real-ocean environment.
Photographers & Wildlife Lovers
Capture the magic of sea turtles, blue whales, reef fish, and vibrant coral. We offer free 4K GoPro photos and video for group bookings — and our guides know exactly how to find the most photogenic marine encounters.
Our Commitment

Safety, Respect & Responsibility

Your safety and the health of the ocean are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to responsible marine tourism that protects Sri Lanka's underwater heritage for future generations.

Pre-Activity Safety Briefings
Before every activity, our team provides a comprehensive safety briefing and ensures you understand exactly what to expect in the water. No one enters the ocean without being fully prepared and comfortable.
Professional Grade Equipment
We use high-quality, regularly serviced snorkeling and scuba diving equipment maintained to industry standards. From BCDs and regulators to masks and wetsuits — everything is inspected before every use.
PADI-Certified Instructors & Guides
Every member of our team holds internationally recognised PADI certifications. Our dive masters and instructors bring years of practical experience guiding guests of all abilities safely through Mirissa's waters.
Responsible Marine Tourism
We actively educate our guests on how to interact respectfully with marine life — never touching, chasing, or disturbing animals. We are committed to preserving the ocean ecosystem that makes these experiences possible.
Inclusive & Welcoming for All
We go out of our way to make every guest feel welcome — non-swimmers, first-timers, families with children, elderly visitors, and solo adventurers alike. The ocean is for everyone, and we make sure everyone can experience it.
Deep Local Knowledge
Our team has spent years diving the waters around Mirissa and knows the best spots for every kind of marine encounter. This local expertise means higher sighting rates, safer conditions, and a richer experience for every guest.

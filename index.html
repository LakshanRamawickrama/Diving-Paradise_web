Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your ocean tour.

Can non-swimmers participate in snorkeling?
Yes, absolutely! We provide floating life jackets and lifebuoys, and our experienced guides will swim right alongside non-swimmers to ensure you feel 100% safe while seeing the turtles.
What equipment is provided during the tour?
We provide all necessary sanitized gear including high-definition mask, snorkel tube, fins, and life jackets. For scuba diving, complete tank and regulator sets are included.
How do I get the GoPro underwater photos?
Our guides capture HD underwater photos and video clips during the tour. After returning to shore, we transfer all original files directly to your phone via AirDrop, WhatsApp, or Google Drive for free!
What happens if we don't spot blue whales?
While sighting success rates are very high during peak season, if whales are not encountered due to sea conditions, we offer a complimentary 'Snorkeling with Sea Turtles' package to ensure you still have an amazing day!
Where are you located in Mirissa?
We are located at No. 21 Athkam Niwasa, Thappawatt Road, Mirissa Beach (Southern Province, Sri Lanka). We also offer free hotel pick-up and drop-off in Mirissa and Weligama.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your ocean tour.

Can non-swimmers participate in snorkeling?
Yes, absolutely! We provide floating life jackets and lifebuoys, and our experienced guides will swim right alongside non-swimmers to ensure you feel 100% safe while seeing the turtles.
What equipment is provided during the tour?
We provide all necessary sanitized gear including high-definition mask, snorkel tube, fins, and life jackets. For scuba diving, complete tank and regulator sets are included.
Are GoPro photos & videos free for everyone?
Free HD underwater photos and video clips are captured exclusively for group bookings (2 or more guests). Our guides capture group memories during the tour and transfer all files to your phone after returning to shore via AirDrop, WhatsApp, or Google Drive. Note: Free photo & video capture is provided only for groups.
What happens if we don't spot blue whales?
While sighting success rates are very high during peak season, if whales are not encountered due to sea conditions, we offer a complimentary 'Snorkeling with Sea Turtles' package to ensure you still have an amazing day!
Where are you located in Mirissa?
We are located at No.21 Athkam Niwasa, Mirissa (Southern Province, Sri Lanka). We can also arrange hotel pick-up and drop-off in Mirissa and Weligama.

What is Discover Scuba Diving?

Discover Scuba Diving is a "try scuba" experience for people who have never dived before and donâ€™t have a diving license.

How it works:

  1. No experience or certificate needed: We teach you everything. No previous diving required.
  2. Quick training session: About 30 minutes. You learn basic skills with an instructor in shallow water or a pool (breathing underwater, hand signals, safety rules).
  3. 1 Open water dive: Then you go into the ocean with an instructor, 1-on-1. Max depth is usually 12 meters / 40 feet. The instructor stays with you the whole time.
  4. Takes about 3-4 hours total: Training + 1 dive. You can do it in half a day.

Who is it for?

Perfect for tourists who want to try scuba diving for the first time without doing a full course. Itâ€™s one of the most popular activities at Diving Paradise Mirissa.

Important to know:

With Discover Scuba you do NOT get a certification. If you want a license to dive anywhere in the world, you need to do the PADI Open Water Diver Course.
